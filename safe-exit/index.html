If it is hard right now, call.

In danger now: 112 in Kazakhstan and Europe, 911 in the US.

Safe Exit lists checked crisis helplines by country. Other agentic websites can ask it for numbers. They send only a country code, never a name or a story.

  • 150 trust line for children and young people, Mon to Fri 9:00 to 18:00, WhatsApp +7 708 106 08 10

How other sites use it

  1. A site lists Safe Exit in its links.json under a name, for example safe_exit.
  2. Before the first call, the visitor sees a consent card and chooses to allow it.
  3. The caller's agent sends one field, country. The site's rule blocks everything else.
  4. Safe Exit runs in the visitor's browser on the caller's model. It holds no keys and keeps no logs.
