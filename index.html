Between you and your goal there are steps.

The Ladder is a self-check by Aizhan Li, author of the Ladder method. Name one goal, say where you are now and which words hold you back. The assistant draws your ladder.

Nothing is stored. The agents run in this browser; your answers go only to the AI model and to the Ladder reader at 108psy.com, which does not keep them. The panel below shows every request as it happens.
